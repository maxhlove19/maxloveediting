A simple monthly rate with no contract, and you can stop any time. Ask, and I’ll send this month’s rate along with your free clip, so you can judge the work before the price.

Only send the link to where your service is posted. There’s nothing to film differently, no software, and no meetings — everything happens by email.

Yes. Tell me a moment you want, a series you’re in, or anything to leave out, and the clips follow that.

The clips come to you ready to post, with a suggested caption for each, so your church stays in control of its own pages.
